**Before installing**

- **Head units that check the phone's certificate will refuse it.** This build carries our self-signed development certificate: it works with head units that don't check it (Crankshaft/OpenAuto, for instance). Factory head units that verify it against Google's refuse the connection. The app says so the first time it starts.
- **Shizuku is optional but recommended.** With it, the music is moved to the car like the official app does and the car is connected for calls; without it the sound is copied, after a consent prompt. The app tells you by notification when Shizuku stops.

Setup, limits and debugging: see the [README](https://github.com/simoneluconi/BetterAuto#readme).
